DICT & DepEd Training Programs

Facilitating digital transformation initiatives, ICT capacity building, and specialized skill tracks for educators, students, and public sector personnel.

Project Overview

The DICT & DepEd Training Programs represent strategic government-backed initiatives focused on strengthening digital literacy, technical competencies, and workforce readiness across public schools and regional communities.

Through structured workshops, hands-on ICT laboratories, and virtual assistant skill tracks, these programs bridge the digital divide by equipping teachers, administrators, and aspiring remote workers with industry-relevant digital capabilities.

Key Deliverables & Facilitation

  • ICT & Digital Skill Training: Conducted technical modules covering office productivity, cloud collaboration, and digital administrative workflows.
  • Educator Upskilling Workshops: Trained teaching staff on integrating digital tools and online management systems into instructional environments.
  • Specialized Technical Track Support: Assisted in delivering focused training tracks including Virtual Executive Assistance, Web Administration, and SEO basics.
  • Resource Material Development: Created practical guides, step-by-step documentation, and lab exercises tailored for public sector participants.

Results & Measured Impact

Strengthened community digital readiness and administrative capability within public sector institutions.

  • Prepared local participants for remote employment opportunities through standardized digital skill modules.
  • Enhanced multi-agency institutional cooperation between ICT authorities and regional educational divisions.
