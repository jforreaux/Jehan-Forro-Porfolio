ASEAN Women MSMEs Empowerment Program

Facilitating regional digital transformation initiatives and mentoring local women entrepreneurs to pitch and scale on international platforms.

Project Overview

The ASEAN Women MSMEs Empowerment Program is a regional initiative designed to accelerate digital literacy, e-commerce integration, and modern business management techniques among women-led micro, small, and medium enterprises.

Through interactive workshops, hands-on digital tools training, and tailored one-on-one business coaching, the project bridge digital adoption gaps and prepares regional business owners for competitive market opportunities across Southeast Asia.

Key Deliverables & Facilitation

  • Workshop Facilitation: Delivered capacity-building modules covering digital marketing, store operations, and workflow automation.
  • 1-on-1 Business Coaching: Conducted mentorship sessions focusing on pitch deck preparation, business model refinement, and brand positioning.
  • Digital Tool Adoption: Guided participants through productivity software, e-commerce platforms, and customer management systems.
  • International Delegate Prep: Supported candidate evaluation and coached selected entrepreneurs for high-stakes regional showcases.

Results & Measured Impact

Elevated participant business readiness and achieved top regional recognition for mentored enterprises.
