Project Overview

As a Trainer and Mentor for the ASEAN Women MSMEs (IDEAS) Project, implemented by the Asia-Pacific Women's Information Network Center (APWINC), I facilitated capacity-building workshops on digital transformation and e-commerce expansion.

Supported by the ASEAN-Korea Cooperation Fund (AKCF), this initiative focused on empowering female entrepreneurs with modern digital toolkits, strategic e-commerce practices, and cross-border commercial opportunities.

A major milestone included providing targeted mentorship to prepare 1 of the 4 Philippine representative MSMEs for participation in the prestigious ASEAN Women MSME Acceleration Expo in Seoul, South Korea in August 2026.

Key Deliverables & Facilitation

  • Digital & E-Commerce Workshops: Facilitated comprehensive training modules covering e-commerce platforms, digital marketing, and business digitization for regional MSMEs.
  • Cross-Border Strategy Mentorship: Prepared local businesses for global trade dynamics and strategic collaboration with South Korean and regional stakeholders.
  • High-Impact Business Coaching: Mentored a featured Philippine women-led MSME, sharpening their pitch, digital storefront, and operational workflow for international exposure.
  • Regional Capacity Building: Supported sustainable digital adoption to foster gender-inclusive economic growth across ASEAN markets.
