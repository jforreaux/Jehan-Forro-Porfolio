Project Overview

As a Trainer for the AI Ready ASEAN Program implemented by Limitless Lab, I delivered the AI Readiness Course to equip youth and small business owners with essential artificial intelligence skills.

In collaboration with JCI Malagos Davao on Project WISE (Work Smarter, Market Smarter with AI), the initiative focused on providing hands-on AI training to students and local entrepreneurs, generating new digital economic opportunities across the community.

By combining foundational and advanced AI literacy with practical marketing and productivity workflows, participants gained actionable skills to innovate their everyday operations.

Key Deliverables & Facilitation

  • Dynamic Training Workshops: Facilitated interactive sessions empowering students and entrepreneurs with both foundational and advanced AI literacy.
  • Project WISE Collaboration: Partnered with JCI Malagos Davao on "Work Smarter, Market Smarter with AI" to bring AI productivity strategies to small business owners.
  • Digital Economic Empowerment: Guided participants on leveraging AI tools to optimize local business marketing, automate operations, and expand digital reach.
  • Ethical & Practical AI Integration: Educated learners on prompt design, responsible AI usage, and real-world implementation across academic and business workflows.
