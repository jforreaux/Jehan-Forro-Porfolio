AI Literacy & Digital Enablement ASEAN Initiative

AI Ready ASEAN Program

Democratizing artificial intelligence skills across Southeast Asia through practical training, generative AI tool adoption, and workforce readiness workshops.

Project Overview

The AI Ready ASEAN Program is a regional capacity-building initiative aimed at bridging the artificial intelligence knowledge gap for youth, educators, and working professionals across ASEAN member states.

By combining foundational AI literacy with hands-on practice in prompt engineering, automated workflows, and ethical AI usage, the program empowers local communities to integrate modern AI solutions into everyday productivity and administrative operations.

Key Deliverables & Facilitation

  • AI Literacy Workshops: Led interactive training modules covering foundational AI concepts, prompt design, and practical workplace applications.
  • Generative AI Tool Training: Guided participants in leveraging large language models and automation tools to streamline document creation and data analysis.
  • Ethical AI Frameworks: Facilitated discussions on responsible AI adoption, data privacy, and mitigation of algorithmic bias.
  • Curriculum Adaptation: Developed localized learning materials and exercise templates for non-technical audiences and community leaders.
