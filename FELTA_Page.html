FELTA Educational Technology Initiatives

Advancing ed-tech solutions, interactive STEM learning, and school-wide digital transformations through specialized teacher training and curriculum integration.

Project Overview

In collaboration with FELTA Multi-Media Inc., a pioneer in educational technology in the Philippines, this initiative focuses on modernizing school classrooms through hands-on STEM tools, robotics education, and interactive learning media.

The program bridges modern ed-tech solutions with school curricula, equipping educators with practical skills to integrate digital tools, robotic kits, and interactive software into everyday teaching.

Key Deliverables & Facilitation

  • STEM & Robotics Workshops: Facilitated hands-on training for educators on implementing robotics and interactive learning platforms.
  • Curriculum Integration: Designed framework guides to seamlessly embed modern educational technology into school learning modules.
  • Resource Speaker Delivery: Conducted large-scale webinars and live demonstrations highlighting school-wide tech transformation.
  • Technical Consultation: Assisted educational institutions in evaluating and deploying learning technology tools suited for their students.

Transformed classroom tech integration and empowered educators across various academic institutions.
