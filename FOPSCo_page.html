Project Overview

As a member of the Board of Directors (BOD) and Business Center Manager for the Filipino Online Professionals Service Cooperative (FOPSCo), I exercise strategic governance and final approval authority over key organizational policies and development initiatives.

My role centers on formulating and enforcing robust governance guidelines for social media, external communications, content moderation, and operational frameworks to safeguard organizational integrity and foster sustainable cooperative growth.

Additionally, I lead the oversight and implementation of the Gender and Development (GAD) plan, ensuring transparent progress tracking, equity, and accountability across all member-driven programs and operations.

Key Deliverables & Executive Responsibilities

  • Policy Formulation & Enforcement: Authored and implemented governance guidelines for social media and communications to maintain brand consistency and risk compliance.
  • Proposal Authorization & Oversight: Evaluated and authorized formal operational proposals covering moderation, workflow structures, and strategic expansion.
  • Gender and Development (GAD) Leadership: Directed the GAD framework execution and conducted annual progress evaluations to ensure equity and institutional transparency.
  • Democratic & Member-Centric Governance: Provided high-level leadership for member-driven initiatives, promoting equitable participation and long-term financial sustainability.
